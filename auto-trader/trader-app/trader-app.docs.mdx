The dashboard is the control surface for the autonomous trading engine. It polls
the trading service every few seconds and shows what the engine is doing and why.

## What it shows

- **Account health** — equity, free balance, open P&L, win rate, profit factor and
  current drawdown against its limit.
- **Open positions** — direction, leverage, collateral and live P&L, with a
  stop/entry/target bar showing where price sits inside the trade, plus the
  reasons the engine took it.
- **Scanner ranking** — every market the engine scored this cycle, with its
  direction, regime and conviction against the entry threshold.
- **Engine log** — a running record of every open, close and risk event.
- **Risk controls** — the guardrails that govern sizing and leverage, editable at
  runtime.

## Controls

Start and pause the loop, force an immediate scan, close any position at market,
or reset the paper account back to its starting balance.

## Running it

The dashboard needs the trading service. Run both together through the platform:

```bash
bit run trader-platform
```

In development the API is proxied through the frontend origin, so no CORS setup is
needed. In production the app calls the gateway directly through `BACKEND_URL`,
which the platform injects.

> Paper trading against live market data. No real orders are placed, and nothing
> here is financial advice.
