The trading service is a self-contained autonomous trading engine. Every cycle it
scans live perpetual futures markets, scores each one, opens the positions that
clear its conviction threshold, and manages the exits of everything already open.

Execution is **paper trading against live market prices** — no venue credentials,
no real orders. The execution boundary is isolated, so replacing it with a broker
adapter for a venue you are permitted to trade on is a contained change.

## How a trade is decided

The engine makes every decision on its own, in this order:

1. **Which market** — the most liquid USDT perpetuals, ranked by how much they are
   actually moving. Non-crypto contracts are excluded.
2. **Which direction** — a blend of a trend model (EMA spread, MACD, momentum) and
   a mean-reversion model (RSI, channel position), weighted by the detected market
   regime. ADX scales the resulting conviction rather than voting on direction, and
   funding rate adds a crowding bias.
3. **Whether to trade at all** — see the entry checks below. This is what stops the
   engine taking a position just because something moved.
4. **How big** — the stop defines the risk, never the leverage. A risk budget is
   derived from equity and conviction, then divided by the stop distance.
5. **How much leverage** — the highest level that is still safe, capped so the stop
   loss always triggers before the liquidation price, and capped again in volatile
   markets.

## Entry checks

A good score is not enough. Each setup is put through named conditions, and the
result is attached to the signal so the dashboard can show exactly why a trade was
taken or skipped.

| Check | What it asks | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Higher timeframe | Does the 1h trend oppose this trade? | **Veto** |
| No exhausted move | Has price covered more than 2.5 ATR in 3 bars? | **Veto** |
| Consistent direction | Is the regression slope going the same way? | Lowers conviction |
| Volume confirmation | Did the last closed candle trade near its average? | Lowers conviction |
| Room to structure | Is the nearest opposing level at least 1.2R away? | Lowers conviction |
| Tradeable regime | Is the market something other than chop? | Lowers conviction |

A setup the higher timeframe actively confirms gets a conviction bonus and keeps a
runner in its profit ladder.

## Exits

Exits are staged rather than all-or-nothing. Each position carries a ladder of
profit targets, and the engine closes a tranche at each one:

- **Trending, confirmed by the 1h** — 35% at 1.2R, 35% at 2.5R, 30% at 4R.
- **Trending, unconfirmed** — 40% at 1.2R, 60% at 2.2R.
- **Range** — 60% at 1R, 40% at 1.8R, because price is expected to turn at the edge.

The first fill moves the stop to entry, so a winner can no longer become a loser.
Beyond the ladder, a position exits on its stop, on a trailing stop that arms at
+1R, when the scanner flips to the opposite side with conviction, on maximum age,
or on the liquidation guard.

Stops are anchored on market structure where possible: when a confirmed swing sits
a sensible distance away, the stop goes behind that level instead of on a pure
volatility band — price then has to break something real to take the trade out.

## Risk guardrails

Every entry passes portfolio gates before it is allowed: a maximum number of
concurrent positions, a cap on total margin deployed, a maximum drawdown, and a
daily loss limit. Exits are handled by a stop loss, a conviction-scaled target, a
trailing stop that arms at +1R, a maximum position age, and a liquidation guard.
A position can never lose more than the collateral behind it.

## Usage

```ts
import { TradingService } from '@minecraft7900/auto-trader.trading-service';

const service = TradingService.from();
await service.init();

const snapshot = await service.snapshot();
console.log(snapshot.account.equity, snapshot.open.length);
```

Adjust the risk profile at runtime:

```ts
service.updateRisk({ baseRiskPct: 0.005, maxLeverage: 10, maxOpenPositions: 3 });
```

Score a single market directly, without the engine:

```ts
import { buildSignal, planTrade, DEFAULT_RISK } from '@minecraft7900/auto-trader.trading-service';

const signal = buildSignal(ticker, candles);
const plan = signal ? planTrade(signal, account, DEFAULT_RISK) : null;
// plan.leverage, plan.margin, plan.stopLoss, plan.takeProfit
```

## HTTP API

| Method | Path | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `GET` | `/snapshot` | Account, positions, signals, events and stats in one payload |
| `POST` | `/engine/start` · `/engine/stop` | Control the autonomous loop |
| `POST` | `/engine/cycle` | Run a single cycle immediately |
| `POST` | `/risk` | Update risk settings |
| `POST` | `/positions/:id/close` | Force-close a position at market |
| `POST` | `/simulate` | Move one market to a price and run exit management — for verifying take-profit and stop behaviour |
| `POST` | `/reset` | Reset the paper account and wipe history |

## Configuration

| Variable | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `MONGO_URL` | Injected automatically; falls back to in-memory state when absent |
| `PAPER_START_BALANCE` | Starting paper balance, defaults to `10000` |
| `FUTURES_API_BASE` | Market data endpoint, for pointing at another venue |
| `AUTOSTART` | Set to `false` to boot without starting the loop |
